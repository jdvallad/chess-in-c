🧠 Why can Total Loss look flat while the bot gets stronger?

Self-play is a moving target: as both White and Black get stronger, games stay balanced longer, so WDL Loss naturally settles around 0.55–0.68 instead of falling to 0. Always look at Policy Loss falling, Policy Top-1 Acc rising, and head-to-head Elo vs. earlier gens or Zero-C v1!

🎯 Policy Accuracy & Playout Caps

On 25% of moves Zero-C runs a Full Search (300–400 sims) to discover deep tactics, and trains its neural Policy head to predict that search result instantly. When Policy Acc climbs from 10% → 45% → 60%+, Zero-C starts seeing strong candidate moves in 0 sims.

⚖ WDL Value & Moves-Left (Endgame Killer)

Instead of a single score, Zero-C predicts exact Win / Draw / Loss probabilities plus Moves-Left (plies until game end). In winning endgames where every good move looks like +95% Win, the Moves-Left head breaks ties so Zero-C forces the fastest checkmate instead of shuffling.

🚀 What's new in Zero-C v2 (Path A + Path C)?

Training (Pure Tabula Rasa): 10×128 SE-ResNet (3.56M), 20-plane repetition+STM input, 1.2M replay buffer, MCTS subtree reuse, Exact Mate Solver, Q-blended WDL, and smart resignation.
Play Mode (Path C Hybrid): 600+ FP16 sims + subtree reuse + zero-bias capture quiescence delta so it never hangs pieces while keeping its pure neural style.
